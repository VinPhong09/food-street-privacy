About this policy

SoftBear ("we", "us") provides Food Street: Idle Tycoon, also known as Idle Food Street, Android package com.softbear.idlefoodstreet. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game and this privacy website.

For privacy questions, support, or data requests, contact softbears.game@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game

  • Game progress and settings: unlocked streets and stalls, levels, in-game currency, missions, rewards, staff assignments, and preferences are stored on your device. When cloud services are available, a copy of your save and related metadata can be stored using Unity Cloud Save.
  • Player and account information: Unity Authentication assigns a player identifier, including for guest sessions. Sign-in providers, where enabled, process identifiers and authentication information needed to link or restore your game. Cloud-save metadata includes a player ID, save timestamps and revision, a device description, and progress summaries. Do not send us your account password or sign-in codes.
  • Advertising and technical information: the game uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads. Its SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising and other device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These are used for advertising, measurement, service operation, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable privacy choices.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include so we can respond to your request.

Why information is used

Information is used to run the game, save and restore progress, authenticate players, provide and measure rewarded ads, apply privacy choices, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support and privacy requests.

Service providers

Google AdMob provides advertising. Unity Gaming Services provides authentication and cloud-save functionality. Available sign-in providers handle their sign-in processes under their own policies. These services may process information in countries other than your own.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or necessary to address security or fraud, subject to applicable requirements.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local game data is retained on your device until you remove it using Android app-storage controls or other applicable device controls. Removing local game data does not by itself delete an existing cloud save.

Cloud game data is retained to provide the account and save-restoration functionality. You can request deletion through the game's account controls or by contacting us below. Data processed by service providers, including their operational records or backups, is subject to their retention practices and applicable legal requirements. This policy does not promise an immediate purge of every provider record.

Where cloud-account features are available, select DELETE ACCOUNT in the game's account screen to delete the current game account and its cloud save. The game can then continue as a guest while retaining this device's local progress. This action does not delete your separate Google or other sign-in-provider account. To remove local progress too, use Android's app-storage controls.

Request account or data deletion

You can request deletion without opening or reinstalling the game. Email softbears.game@gmail.com with the subject Food Street: Idle Tycoon — Data deletion.

Include your player ID, if available, and enough information to identify the game account. Explain whether you want the account and cloud progress deleted or have another data request. Never send passwords or authentication codes. We may request information necessary to verify ownership before acting. We will explain any information that must be retained for applicable legal or security reasons.

Deletion of the game's account does not automatically delete records that Google or other providers control independently. Their policies explain their own request procedures.

Privacy choices

Where available, SETTINGS → AD PRIVACY lets you review advertising consent choices. Availability depends on your region and the advertising consent system. Android may also provide advertising-privacy controls. If you choose not to watch a rewarded ad, its associated reward is not earned.

Children

Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information or believe a child has provided personal information can contact softbears.game@gmail.com. Please identify the game and provide enough information for us to investigate. We review these requests and address information that must be removed under applicable requirements. Do not send unnecessary information about a child in a support message.

Security and this website

The game uses network services for authentication, cloud saves, and advertising. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee.

This policy website does not include our own analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or sign-in features. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information, including visitor IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its update date when the policy changes. Contact softbears.game@gmail.com for privacy questions, support, and requests about your information.
